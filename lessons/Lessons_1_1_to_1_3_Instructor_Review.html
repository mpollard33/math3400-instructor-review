Symbols and vocabulary
Independent and dependent variables
In y(x), x is the input and y is the function that depends on it. In x(t), t is the input.
y′ and y″
First and second derivatives. When x is independent, y′ = dy/dx and y″ = d²y/dx².
C, C₁ and C₂
Arbitrary constants within one solution family. Each independent integration introduces a new constant.
Initial-value problem (IVP)
A differential equation together with specified values of the function and, when needed, its derivatives at an initial input.
Order
The order of the highest derivative, not a power such as x⁵ or (y′)³.
Linear equation
An equation linear in the dependent variable and its derivatives. Their coefficients may depend on the independent variable.
Slope field
A collection of short segments whose slopes are prescribed by y′ = f(x,y).
Isocline and zero-slope locus
An isocline has one prescribed field slope. A zero-slope locus has slope zero. Neither name by itself means the locus is a solution curve.
Equilibrium solution
A constant solution. Its derivative is zero, and the equation must agree all along that constant function.
ODE and PDE · orientation only
These lessons use ordinary differential equations (ODEs), with one independent variable. Partial differential equations (PDEs) involve partial derivatives of functions of several variables. PDE methods aren’t taught in this lesson.
